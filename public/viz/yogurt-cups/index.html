The yogurt cup game

the printed catalogues and cards
Two people, two laptops. One of you will see five pictures. The other has to find them, in order, in a catalogue of a hundred or more. You may send words, and nothing else. Your score is the time it takes. That is the only score.
1. Both pick the same set of pictures
2. Both pick the same round
3. Decide who does what

Nothing travels between the two laptops by itself: there is no server, and nothing needs one. You both choose the same set and the same round, out loud, and the pages agree from there.

Look at all of them, and agree how you will describe any one. Take as long as you like. Nothing is timed yet, and there is nothing written under the pictures on purpose: whatever you use to name them, the two of you have to invent.
