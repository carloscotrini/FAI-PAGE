Plymouth, 1906

At the West of England Fat Stock and Poultry Exhibition, Francis Galton watched 800 visitors pay sixpence to guess the dressed weight of a slaughtered ox.

Galton was a vox-populi sceptic. He expected the crowd to be wildly off — and ran the analysis to prove it.

The median guess was 1197 lb. The actual weight: 1198 lb.

F. Galton, "Vox Populi", Nature, 7 March 1907.

USS Scorpion, 1968

22 May 1968. The submarine USS Scorpion vanished in the Atlantic with 99 men aboard. Six months of search yielded nothing.

Navy mathematician John Craven gathered submariners, salvage operators, and oceanographers — none of them experts in submarine losses. He asked each, independently, to mark a probability map of the seabed: where do you think the wreck is?

He weighted the maps by domain expertise, applied Bayes' rule across them, and pointed to a single coordinate.

Five months later, the wreck was found about 200 metres from his point estimate — on a search area the size of a small country.

Sherry Sontag & Christopher Drew, Blind Man's Bluff (1998); J. P. Craven, The Silent War (2001).

The catch

Round 2 is tighter than round 1. Often it is no closer to the truth. Sometimes further.

Lorenz, Rauhut, Schweitzer & Helbing measured this with 144 subjects across six estimation tasks: social influence shrinks the spread of guesses (overconfidence) without improving accuracy.

"How social influence can undermine the wisdom of crowd effect", PNAS 108(22), 2011.

Why

Var(Yn) = ρ·σ² + (1−ρ)·σ²/n

The variance of the average of n estimators with pairwise correlation ρ. When ρ = 0, the crowd is independent — variance shrinks like 1/n. When ρ = 1, the crowd agrees — variance stays at σ², no matter how many you add.

So what?

Every ensemble method is a wisdom-of-crowds machine. Their differences come down to ρ.

  • Bagging. Each tree on a bootstrap sample. Trees see different data. Low ρ. Variance shrinks like 1/n.
  • Boosting. Trees fit sequentially on each other's residuals. Highly correlated. ρ near 1. Variance floor at ρσ². Fights bias, not variance.
  • Random forests. Bagging plus per-split feature subsampling. Decorrelates further. ρ even lower. Beats plain bagging.

Independence is engineered, not assumed. The reason your round-2 estimates didn't improve is the same reason boosted trees plateau on variance.
